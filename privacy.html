Introduction

AGilsonDesigns ("we," "our," or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the LifeHub mobile application.

Information We Collect

LifeHub is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. Depending on the features you choose to use, the application may store information that you intentionally provide, such as notes, tasks, reminders, or other personal content.

How Your Information Is Used

  • Save and organize information you choose to enter.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties except when required by law or when necessary to provide services you explicitly choose to use.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to help protect information stored by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may delete your information from within the application at any time, where applicable. Removing the application may also remove locally stored information depending on your device settings.

Children's Privacy

LifeHub is not directed toward children under the age required by applicable law without parental permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date shown at the top of this page.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact AGilsonDesigns through the support page.
